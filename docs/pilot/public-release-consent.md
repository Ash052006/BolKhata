# Consent Form for Public Release of Voice Recordings in Open Research

> **Repository:** BolKhata (बोलखाता)
> **Benchmark Project:** BolKhata Spoken Indian Amounts Benchmark (BSIAB)
> **Applicable Laws:** Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India)

---

## Participant Information & Explanation

You are being invited to voluntarily contribute your voice recordings to an open-source scientific benchmark dataset named **BSIAB (BolKhata Spoken Indian Amounts Benchmark)**.

This form is **separate and distinct** from your previous pilot trial consent. The pilot consent permitted private, internal testing of the BolKhata app. This agreement explicitly governs **public release** of your voice data.

### 1. What Data Will Be Made Public?
- Short audio clips (typically 2 to 5 seconds) of you speaking financial numbers, amounts, and item names (e.g., *"dhai sau rupaye"*, *"do kilo chawal"*).
- Written transcripts and exact numeric values of what was spoken.
- Your assigned anonymous code (e.g., `SPK_03`), your approximate language/dialect, and the noise environment (e.g., *"quiet shop counter"*).

### 2. What Will NEVER Be Shared or Released?
- Your real name, shop name, or business address.
- Your phone number, Aadhaar number, PAN, or financial banking details.
- Any recording that accidentally mentions a customer's real name or phone number.

### 3. Voice Biometrics Risk Disclosure (Plain Language)
> [!WARNING] Important Privacy & Biometric Notice
> Your human voice is unique and considered biometric data. Once audio files are published publicly on the internet (e.g., on Hugging Face, GitHub, or academic data repositories), it is possible for advanced artificial intelligence systems to match your voice across other recordings. While your name will never be attached to the files, public distribution cannot be completely recalled or deleted from copies made by third parties once downloaded.

### 4. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw
- You have the absolute right to decline this public release without affecting your participation in the BolKhata pilot trial or any current services.
- You may ask to delete your recordings from the source benchmark repository at any time prior to public release by contacting the researcher.
